Who she was

Amma Syncletica

of Alexandria · c. 270 – c. 350

  • Born to a wealthy Alexandrian family, and well educated.
  • When her parents died she sold everything and gave it away.
  • She moved with her blind sister to a tomb outside the city. Other women followed her there, and she became their teacher.
  • Twenty-seven of her sayings survive in the Sayings of the Desert Fathers, more than any other woman in that collection.
  • Though a hermit herself, she taught moderation: the asceticism was never the point.

Today she is the one who says you can be alone with God in a crowded room.

The practice

Solitude

Spiritual Disciplines Handbook · Adele Ahlberg Calhoun · Part 3 · Relinquish the False Self

Solitude is not a place. It is something you can carry into a room full of people.

  • ·Close your eyes and listen to the room
  • ·Let each sound go past without following it
  • ·Now let the room recede, and be alone with God inside it
