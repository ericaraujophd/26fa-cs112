Who she was

Amma Theodora

of Alexandria · 4th century

  • Married to a Roman officer. After his death she went to the desert.
  • She became amma — spiritual mother — of a community of women near Alexandria.
  • The Desert Fathers came to her for counsel, and so did Theophilus, the bishop of Alexandria.
  • Her sayings are short, blunt and often funny. She teaches by telling stories against the ego.

Today she is the one who asked the demons what actually drives them away.
