Who she was

Macrina the Younger

Cappadocia · c. 327 – 379

  • The eldest of ten children. Her brothers were Basil the Great and Gregory of Nyssa — the two who wrote her down.
  • Betrothed at twelve. Her fiancé died before the wedding and she refused to marry anyone else.
  • She turned the family estate at Annisa into a monastic community where the household’s servants lived as equals with the family.
  • Gregory says she took Basil’s pride in his own cleverness apart and turned him toward the ascetic life. She was the theologian who taught the theologians.
  • Gregory was at her deathbed, and wrote down her last conversation and her prayer.

Today she is the one who prayed her way out of this life in complete sentences.
