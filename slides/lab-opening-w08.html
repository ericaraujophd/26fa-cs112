Who she was

Vibia Perpetua

Carthage · c. 181 – 203

  • A young noblewoman, about twenty-two, married, nursing an infant son.
  • Arrested as a catechumen under Septimius Severus. She was baptized after her arrest.
  • She kept a prison diary — one of the earliest surviving texts written by a Christian woman. We have her own words, not a summary of them.
  • Her father came again and again and begged her to recant. She would not.
  • She was killed in the arena on the emperor’s birthday, 7 March 203.

Today she is the one who could not call a thing by any name but its own.
