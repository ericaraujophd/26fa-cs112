Who she was

Amma Sarah

of the Desert · 4th – 5th century

  • She lived sixty years as a hermit in a cell above the Nile.
  • She fought one temptation for thirteen years, and never once prayed for it to stop — only for strength.
  • Two monks came out to humble her. She answered: “According to nature I am a woman, but not according to my thoughts.”

Today she is the one who refused to pray to be liked.

The practice

Examen

Spiritual Disciplines Handbook · Adele Ahlberg Calhoun · Part 2 · Open Myself to God

The examen looks back over real hours and asks what was going on inside them.

  • ·Bring last week’s work to mind — the actual hours you spent
  • ·Ask: for whom did I do that?
  • ·Notice what you find, without scolding yourself for it
  • ·Offer it back
